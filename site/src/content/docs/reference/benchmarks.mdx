In a matched, frozen LitSearch run, Scholar's top-five hit rate is ten points above Exa's paper search, and six points above at top twenty.

<Figure src={quality} alt="LitSearch hit rates for Scholar, Exa and BM25 at k = 5, 10 and 20" />

| System | Hit@5 | Hit@10 | Hit@20 | MRR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Scholar** | **0.62** | **0.68** | **0.70** | **0.442** |
| Exa `research paper` | 0.52 | 0.58 | 0.64 | 0.435 |
| BM25 `title + abstract` | 0.46 | 0.46 | 0.56 | 0.335 |

In the top five, Scholar hit nine queries that Exa missed, and Exa hit four that Scholar missed.

## Metrics

Hit@k is the share of queries with at least one ground-truth paper in the top k. Recall@k is different, it measures how much of all relevant papers each query recovers. MRR averages the reciprocal rank of the first match, with a miss counted as zero. The frozen summary still uses the old field name `R@k` for hit rates.

## Protocol

All three systems use the same 50 LitSearch inline-ACL queries, ground-truth titles, title matcher and top-20 cutoff.

- **Exa** ran with category `research paper`.
- **Scholar** used its standard retrieval pipeline with Qwen reranking.
- **BM25** follows the official LitSearch title and abstract implementation: lowercase tokenization, English stopword removal, Porter stemming and `BM25Okapi` over the 64K-paper corpus.

The Scholar and Exa runs were collected on 12 May 2026; BM25 was reproduced on 25 August 2026.
